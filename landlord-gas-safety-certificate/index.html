The rules

What landlords have to do

Under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, landlords must:

  • Have every gas appliance, fitting and flue checked every 12 months
  • Use a Gas Safe registered engineer
  • Give tenants a copy within 28 days of the check
  • Give new tenants a copy before they move in
  • Keep a record of each check for at least 2 years
