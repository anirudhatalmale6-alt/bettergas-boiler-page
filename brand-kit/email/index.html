Better Gas email templates

How to use in Brevo

  1. Click Copy email code under the email you want.
  2. Brevo > Marketing > Campaigns > Create campaign > Email.
  3. Sender: Better Gas, info@bettergas.co.uk. Recipients: pick your list.
  4. Design > Code your own (or "Paste your code") > select all > paste > Save.
  5. Send a test email to yourself first, then schedule.

A) For businesses: agents, landlords, property managers

B) For your past customers: boiler service reminder
