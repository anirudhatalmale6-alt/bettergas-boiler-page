Is it time for a new boiler?

Not always. But these are the usual signs that repairing it is just delaying the bill.

It's over 10-15 years oldOlder boilers waste more gas and parts get harder to find.
It keeps breaking downTwo or three call-outs a year soon cost more than a new one.
Your bills keep going upA modern boiler can use noticeably less gas for the same heat.
Hot water that comes and goesFluctuating temperature is often a sign of wear inside.

Smell gas? Leave the property, don't use switches, and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999 straight away.
