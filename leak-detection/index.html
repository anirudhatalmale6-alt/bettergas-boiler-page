Signs of a leak

What to look out for

Leaks often hide under floors or behind walls for a long time before they show. These are the usual clues.

  • Boiler keeps losing pressure
  • Damp patches or stains on walls and ceilings
  • Water meter moving with every tap off
  • Water bill higher than normal
  • Sound of running water when nothing is on
  • Warm patches on the floor
