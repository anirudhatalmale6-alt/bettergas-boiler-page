These steps can stop a lot of damage in the meantime.

  • Turn off the water at the stopcock (often under the kitchen sink)
  • Switch off electrics near the leak if it's safe to reach the fuse box
  • Open the taps to drain the pipes
  • Move valuables and put down towels
  • Take a few photos for your insurance
