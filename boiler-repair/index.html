Before you call

A couple of quick checks that sometimes get the heating back on.

Check the pressureMost boilers like 1 to 1.5 bar on the gauge when cold.
Check the powerIs the boiler display on? Check the fused switch next to it.
Check the thermostatTurn it up a few degrees and make sure the timer is on.
Frozen pipe in winter?A frozen condensate pipe outside is a very common cause.

Smell gas? Leave the property, don't use switches, and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999 straight away.
